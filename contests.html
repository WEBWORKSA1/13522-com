Official rules (summary)

  • No purchase necessary. Purchasing or donating does not improve your chances of winning.
  • Open to individuals 18+ (or the age of majority in their jurisdiction). Void where prohibited or restricted by law.
  • Each challenge runs from the first to the last day of the calendar month. One entry per person per category.
  • Consistency-draw winners are chosen at random from eligible entries; reflection and setup winners are chosen by editors on originality, usefulness and clarity.
  • Winning-day counts are self-reported; we may ask for a screenshot or exported plan to verify.
  • Winners are notified by email within 14 days of the month's end and must reply within 14 days or an alternate winner may be selected.
  • Prizes are non-transferable and have no cash alternative unless stated. Winners are responsible for any taxes.
  • By entering a reflection or setup, you grant 13522.com permission to publish it (with or without your name, as you choose).
  • 13522.com may modify or cancel a challenge if it cannot run as planned. The challenge is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by Google or any app mentioned on this site.
