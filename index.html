Why it works

Nine tasks you can actually finish beats thirty you won't

Long to-do lists create decision fatigue and a permanent sense of being behind. A fixed shape forces the hard choice up front: what matters most today?

🎯

Forced prioritisation

One Big slot means you must decide what the day is for. Everything else is supporting cast.

⚖️

Realistic load

Roughly 2h + 3h + 1h of work fits a real day with meetings, breaks and interruptions.

⏱️

Protected focus

Two timed sprints turn intentions into output. Most people get their Big task done in sprint one.

🔁

Built-in learning

Morning and evening reviews close the loop, so tomorrow's plan is better than today's.

📈

Visible progress

A daily score and streak make consistency tangible — 60% or more counts as a winning day.

🤝

Team-ready

Questions

What does 13522 stand for?

1 big task, 3 medium tasks, 5 small tasks, 2 focus sprints and 2 reviews. It extends the well-known 1-3-5 to-do list with the two habits that make it stick: protected focus time and a short daily review.

Is the planner really free? Where is my data stored?

Yes. The planner runs entirely in your browser and saves to your device's local storage. We don't have an account system and never see your tasks.

What if I have more than nine tasks?

You almost always do. The rest stay on a master list (or in "Parked" after Smart Sort). The point is to choose the nine that make today count — new urgent work replaces a medium task rather than growing the list.

How long should focus sprints be?

Start with 25 minutes if focus is hard right now, 50 minutes for most knowledge work, and 90 minutes for deep creative or technical work. Two sprints a day is the minimum that reliably moves a Big task.

Can I use this with Todoist, Notion or Trello?

Yes — see the tools directory for setup ideas in each app, and the printable templates if you prefer paper.
