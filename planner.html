How to use the planner

  1. Brain dump (2 min). Paste everything on your mind into Smart Sort, or type directly into the slots. Add minutes where you know them.
  2. Choose your Big task. Ask: "If I only finish one thing today, which one makes the biggest difference?" Write it as a finish line, not an activity.
  3. Fill three Medium and five Small. Medium tasks are 30–90 minutes; small ones are under 15. Anything that doesn't fit is parked, not lost.
  4. Morning review. Write one sentence: what will make today a win, and what might get in the way.
  5. Run sprint one on the Big task. Notifications off, one tab open. Choose 25, 50 or 90 minutes.
  6. Batch the small tasks. Clear all five in one block, ideally after lunch when energy dips.
  7. Sprint two on the Big task if unfinished, otherwise on your most important Medium task.
  8. Evening review. Tick what's done, note what carries over and why. The planner moves unfinished items to tomorrow automatically.
Scoring: Big task = 40 points, each Medium = 10, each Small = 4, each sprint = 5. A day of 60% or more counts toward your streak — that's the Big task plus two Medium tasks, for example.
