Team FAQ

Do we need new software?

No. 13522 works in Asana, ClickUp, Notion, Trello, Todoist, Microsoft To Do, or a shared doc. The free plan includes setup notes for common tools.

Is this surveillance?

No. People share one priority, not their screen time. The method is built on trust and clarity, not monitoring.

How long does a rollout take?

Most teams are running it within a week. Two weeks of daily use is enough to see whether carry-overs and meeting load are improving.

What does a workshop cost?

It depends on team size and format. Request one through the form and we'll send a quote with no obligation.
