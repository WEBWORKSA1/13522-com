Free productivity calculators

Put real numbers on time, meetings and focus — then plan your 13522 day around them.

Meeting cost calculator

Meetings are the most expensive line item that never appears on a budget. Multiply people × hourly cost × duration and the number usually surprises. In a 13522 day, a 60-minute meeting occupies a Medium slot — so plan it honestly, and send an agenda so it ends early.

What is my hour worth?

Knowing your hourly value makes delegation decisions obvious. If a small task costs you more than it would cost to automate or outsource, move it off your list permanently.

Task time estimator

People consistently underestimate how long their own tasks take — psychologists call it the planning fallacy. A simple buffer based on your confidence gets you closer to reality and tells you which tier the task belongs in.

Daily capacity check

On meeting-heavy days a full 13522 won't fit. Scale down to a 1·2·3 (one big, two medium, three small) rather than carrying over half your list.

Cost of procrastination

Drift rarely feels expensive in the moment. Annualised, it often equals a holiday, a course or a new laptop. The fix isn't more willpower — it's a pre-decided first task and a timer.

Team focus cost

Unclear priorities, context switching and status-chasing add up across a team. Shared daily Big tasks and short reviews recover a meaningful share of that time.
